## 1. Demo Ecosystems Overview

Ecosystem Rewind includes two distinct, realistically parameterized ecological regions for Phase 1 & 2:

### Ecosystem 1: Western Ghats Evergreen Corridor (`western_ghats_forest`)
- **Type**: Tropical Montane Moist Evergreen Forest & Shola-Grassland Mosaic
- **Coordinates**: Lat 10.1500° N, Long 77.0500° E (Anamalai-Periyar Range, Western Ghats biodiversity hotspot)
- **Area**: 42,500 hectares
- **Baseline State**: Moderate fragmentation due to legacy tea/coffee plantations and linear infrastructure. Moderate pollinator network, high canopy bird diversity, vulnerable apex predator connectivity.
- **Key Species Groups**:
  - *Canopy Flora*: Cullenia exarillata (Karani tree), Mesua ferrea (Ironwood), Ficus microcarpa (Strangler fig)
  - *Pollinators*: Apis dorsata (Giant honey bee), Troides minos (Southern birdwing butterfly)
  - *Avifauna*: Buceros bicornis (Great hornbill), Montecincla cachinnans (Nilgiri laughingthrush)
  - *Mammals / Keystone*: Panthera pardus fusca (Indian leopard), Macaca silenus (Lion-tailed macaque), Elephas maximus (Asian elephant)
  - *Decomposers*: Macrotermes estherae (Termite), Ganoderma lucidum (Bracket fungus)

### Ecosystem 2: Sundarbans Mangrove Tidal Wetland (`sundarbans_wetland`)
- **Type**: Coastal Mangrove & Estuarine Tidal Wetland
- **Coordinates**: Lat 21.9500° N, Long 88.8500° E (Ganges-Brahmaputra-Meghna Delta)
- **Area**: 65,000 hectares
- **Baseline State**: Salinity stress, cyclonic buffer degradation, siltation, high aquatic-benthic food web interdependence, vulnerable mangrove cover.
- **Key Species Groups**:
  - *Halophytic Flora*: Heritiera fomes (Sundari tree), Avicennia marina (Grey mangrove), Rhizophora mucronata (Asiatic mangrove)
  - *Invertebrates / Benthic*: Scylla serrata (Mud crab), Penaeus monodon (Giant tiger prawn), Uca rosea (Fiddler crab)
  - *Avifauna*: Haliaeetus leucogaster (White-bellied sea eagle), Alcedo atthis (Common kingfisher)
  - *Aquatic & Apex Predators*: Panthera tigris tigris (Royal Bengal tiger), Crocodylus porosus (Saltwater crocodile), Platanista gangetica (Ganges river dolphin)
  - *Primary Producers / Phytoplankton*: Skeletonema costatum (Marine diatom)

## 2. Provenance & Scientific Disclaimers
1. **Illustrative Benchmark**: In accordance with project requirements, all observation frequencies, graph interaction weights, cost estimates, and scenario trends are curated demo data modeled on ecological literature.
2. **Deterministic & Repeatable**: The dataset contains zero randomly generated artifacts. Identical inputs yield identical graph metrics and simulations.
3. **No Unverified Claims**: Metric scores represent project-defined indices (0-100) combining normalized biodiversity, canopy cover, pollinator connectivity, and structural integrity.
